# Terra Mystica Fire & Ice Factions

6 factions added by the Fire & Ice expansion: 2 Ice, 2 Volcano, 2 Variable.

**Parser note**: Only Ice factions are parsed. Games with Volcano or Variable factions are skipped.

## Ice Factions (White pieces)

When choosing an Ice faction, you must also immediately choose a **Starting terrain**. This determines:
- Which terrain you place initial structures on (place a free Ice tile first)
- How Spades are counted on the Transformation cycle when terraforming

The chosen Starting terrain may not be the Home terrain of another faction already in play (including Shapeshifters and Riverwalkers). Following players may not choose a faction with that Home terrain.

When transforming terrain into Ice, count Spades from the source terrain to your Starting terrain on the standard Transformation cycle. Transforming your Starting terrain into Ice costs exactly 1 Spade.

### Ice Maidens (Ice)

- **Starting ability**: When choosing Ice Maidens, take 1 Favor tile of your choice (advance cult and gain Power as normal). If the Favor tile has income, receive it from round 1.
- **Stronghold**:
  - Income: standard
  - Ability: When passing each round, gain 3 VP per Temple on the game board

### Yetis (Ice)

- **Starting ability**: Power actions on the game board cost 1 fewer Power. Stronghold and Sanctuary have Power value **4** (instead of 3).
- **Stronghold**:
  - Income: standard
  - Ability: May use Power actions on the game board **even if already taken** (by you or another player). If using one not yet taken, cover with Action token as usual.

## Volcano Factions (Orange pieces)

Choose a Starting terrain **after** each other player has chosen a faction. May not choose the Home or Starting terrain of another faction in play (including Ice Maidens, Yetis, Shapeshifters, and Riverwalkers).

When placing initial structures, place a free Volcano tile on the Starting terrain first. After setup, the Starting terrain has no special meaning.

Volcano factions **never** get VP for placing Volcano tiles on the board (they don't use Spades to transform).

### Dragonlords (Volcano)

- **Starting ability**: Must use **Power tokens** to transform terrain. Power tokens used for terraforming are **removed from the faction board**:
  - 2 Power tokens to transform another player's Home terrain into Volcano
  - 1 Power token for every other terrain type
  - Ice Maidens, Yetis, and Riverwalkers do not have a Home terrain; Ice cannot be transformed
  - Cannot use Spades to transform. Instead, gain 1 Power token from supply into Bowl I per Spade gained
  - If a Scoring tile awards VP for Spades, you still get those VP
- **Stronghold**:
  - Income: standard
  - Ability: Immediately and only once, place Power tokens from the general supply into Bowl I equal to the **number of players**. (Can have >12 Power tokens.)

### Acolytes (Volcano)

- **Starting setup**: Do **not** receive Power for starting cult positions
- **Starting ability**: Must use **Cult points** to transform terrain:
  - 4 Cult points to transform another player's Home terrain into Volcano
  - 3 Cult points for every other terrain type
  - Pay by moving marker down on a **single** Cult track (cannot lose Power by moving down, but can gain it again when moving back up)
  - Ice Maidens, Yetis, and Riverwalkers do not have a Home terrain; Ice cannot be transformed
  - Cannot use Spades to transform. Instead, advance 1 step on a Cult track of choice per Spade gained (different cults allowed for multiple Spades)
  - If a Scoring tile awards VP for Spades, you still get those VP
- **Stronghold**:
  - Income: standard
  - Ability: Advance 1 **more** step for each Priest sent to the order of a Cult (advance 4/3/2 instead of 3/2/1)

## Variable Factions

### Shapeshifters (Variable)

When choosing Shapeshifters, immediately choose a **Home terrain**. May not choose the Home or Starting terrain of another faction already in play (including Ice Maidens and Yetis). Use playing pieces matching the chosen terrain color. Following players may not choose a faction with that Home terrain.

Count Spades for terraforming based on your chosen Home terrain on the standard Transformation cycle.

- **Starting ability**: Standard terraforming using Spades relative to chosen Home terrain
- **Stronghold**:
  - Income: standard
  - Ability: Gain the ability to **change your Home terrain** to any terrain type that is not the Home terrain of another faction in play (Ice Maidens, Yetis, Acolytes, and Dragonlords do not have a Home terrain).
    - Cost: 5 Power (move from Bowl III to Bowl I **or** remove from faction board)
    - This is an Action, can be done multiple times per round (on separate turns)
    - Move the Shapeshifter ring to the new terrain on your Transformation cycle
    - Keep existing playing pieces

### Riverwalkers (Variable)

When choosing Riverwalkers, immediately choose a **Starting terrain**. May not choose the Home or Starting terrain of another faction already in play (including Ice Maidens and Yetis). Use playing pieces matching the chosen terrain color. Following players may not choose a faction with that Home terrain.

Place 1 Priest on each space of your **Terrain cycle** (Transformation cycle) except your Starting terrain.

- **Starting ability**:
  - **No terraforming**: You do not transform terrain. Instead, **unlock terrain types** you can settle in:
    - When you gain a Priest, you may (instead of taking from supply) remove a Priest from your Terrain cycle, return it to supply, and spend coins:
      - 2 coins to unlock a terrain already the Home terrain of another player
      - 1 coin for every other terrain type
    - From then on, you can build on the newly unlocked terrain
    - You can use this whenever you gain a Priest, regardless of how
  - **Cannot use Spades** — they do nothing for Riverwalkers
  - **Structures must be adjacent to River** (initial placements and all subsequent)
  - Subsequent structures must be within reach via Shipping value (boosted by Shipping +1 Bonus card)
  - When founding Towns and during final scoring, structures not connected via Shipping but **directly adjacent** are still considered connected
- **Building ability**: When at least one neighbor takes Power from your building activity:
  - You may pay 1 VP to take 1 Power token from the general supply and place it in **Bowl III** (max 1 per build, regardless of how many neighbors take Power; can have >12 Power tokens)
  - If **all** neighbors refuse Power, gain 1 Power instead (move 1 token per normal rules)
  - If no neighbors at all, gain nothing (same as Cultists)
- **Stronghold**:
  - Income: standard
  - Ability: Immediately and only once, build up to **2 free Bridges**
