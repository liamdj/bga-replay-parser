# Terra Mystica Fan Factions

14 fan factions for the 7 standard terrains. For Ice/Variable/Volcano fan factions, see `terra_mystica_fire_ice_fan_factions.md`.

Standard reference values: starting power 5/7, starting resources 3w+15c, standard building costs as per base game.

---

## Standard Terrain Fan Factions

### Architects (Wasteland) — BGA: "architects"
- **Starting power**: 3/9
- **Starting ability**: Bridges contribute 1 Power toward founding a Town (but don't count as structures for the minimum 4). No Power gain from Bridges when opponents build adjacent; opponents don't gain Power when you place a Bridge. As an action, build 1 Bridge for 1 Priest. When taking Transform and Build to build a Dwelling connected by your Bridge(s), need 1 fewer Spade per Bridge connecting that terrain to your structures (only if building a Dwelling).
- **Stronghold**:
  - Ability: Special action — move 1 of your Bridges to a new valid location and gain 3 VP. Must not break existing Town requirements.

### Archivists (Desert) — BGA: "archivists"
- **Starting setup**: Place 1 additional random Bonus card in the game (4 more Bonus cards than players total)
- **Starting ability**: Do **not** receive Cult bonuses in Phase III. When passing and selecting a new Bonus card, gain 2 Power per Coin on that card.
- **Stronghold**:
  - Ability: When passing, select **2** new Bonus cards (cannot take one you just returned)

### Atlanteans (Lakes) — BGA: "atlanteans"
- **Starting power**: 1/11
- **Starting ability**: Start with Stronghold instead of 2 Dwellings. Placed after all other players place Dwellings. SH is immediately a Town — choose a Town tile at end of placement. Build Bridge for 2 Workers as an action.
- **Stronghold**: When the Town containing the SH reaches these Power value thresholds:
  - **7 Power**: Free Shipping advance (+VP as normal)
  - **10 Power**: +2 on all 4 cult tracks
  - **16 Power**: +20 VP

### Chash Dallah (Forest) — BGA: "chashdallah"
- **Starting ability**: No Exchange track. Has an **Income track** instead. As an action, advance Income track 1 space for 2w+2c, gaining the depicted VP (2/2/3/4 VP for levels 1-4). In Phase I, gain cumulative Coins and Workers from current level and all below.
- **Income track level 0**: 2c basic income (no advance needed)
- **No Spade exchange** — cannot lower Workers-per-Spade cost

### Children of the Wyrm (Swamp) — BGA: "childrenofthewyrm"
- **Starting resources**: 3w, 12c
- **Starting ability**: Gaining Power via opponent building costs 1 fewer VP. When sacrificing Power from Bowl II, each sacrifice moves **2** tokens (instead of 1) from Bowl II to Bowl III. Coin cost of **all** structures (not just TH) depends on opponent adjacency.
- **Stronghold**:
  - On build: regain all removed Power tokens (back to 12 total in Bowl I) + Action token
  - Ability: Special action — place up to 2 Power tokens from any of your Bowls onto River spaces (adjacent to your structures or other Power tokens). Connected structures/spaces via Power token chains count as directly adjacent for you only.

### Conspirators (Mountains) — BGA: "conspirators"
- **Starting ability**: Whenever you take a Favor tile, gain 2 additional Coins.
- **Stronghold**:
  - On build: take a Favor tile + Action token
  - Ability: Special action — swap one of your Favor tiles for a different one. Move matching cult marker down by the Favor tile's cult value (no Power gain/loss from crossing thresholds). Gain normal cult steps, Power, and Coins from the new tile.

### Djinn (Desert) — BGA: "djinni"
- **Starting setup**: 3 Magic Lamp tokens + 2 cult steps on a chosen cult track
- **Starting ability**: As an action, spend 1 Magic Lamp to swap 2 of your cult markers (keeping their levels). Cannot swap to space 10 if another player is there.
- **Stronghold**:
  - Cost: 3w+6c
  - Ability: When passing each round, gain 1 VP per Priest on the Cult board

### Dynion Geifr (Mountains) — BGA: "dyniongeifr"
- **Starting resources**: 2w, 15c
- **Starting setup**: Begin with the Fire-2 Favor tile (+2 Fire cult steps)
- **Starting ability**: Converting a Priest yields 2w+2c (instead of 1w). **All structures have Power value 2** (Dwellings=2, TH=2, TE=2, SH=2, SA=2).
- **Dwelling cost**: 2w+2c
- **Temple income**: p, 5pw, p
- **Base income**: 2w (instead of standard 0w basic income)
- **Stronghold**:
  - On build: gain 2 Priests immediately
  - Ability: Towns including your SH require only **3 structures** (instead of 4)

### The Enlightened (Forest) — BGA: "theenlightened"
- **Starting ability**: Use **Power instead of Workers** to transform terrain (Exchange track shows Power-per-Spade). Anytime during your turn, spend 1 Coin to gain 1 Power token in Bowl I.
- **Dwelling income**: 2pw per slot (instead of 1w)
- **Temple cost**: 1p+5c
- **Sanctuary cost**: 2p+6c
- **Trading House income**: 2c+1pw, 2c+1pw, 3c+1pw, 4c+1pw
- **Stronghold cost**: 2p 4c
- **Stronghold**:
  - Ability: Conversions (Power → Priest/Worker/Coin) yield **2 instead of 1**. Special action: gain 4 Power.

### Goblins (Swamp) — BGA: "goblins"
- **Starting setup**: 1 Treasure token
- **Starting ability**: As an action, spend 1 Treasure token to choose a Structure type and gain rewards per structure of that type on the board: DW→1 Power each, TH→2 Coins each, TE→1 Worker each, SH→1 cult step, SA→2 cult steps. Gain 1 Treasure token when building Temple or Sanctuary (in addition to Favor tile).
- **Stronghold**:
  - Ability: Gain 1 Treasure token when founding a Town (in addition to Town tile)

### Prospectors (Plains) — BGA: "golddiggers"
- **Starting power**: 8/4
- **Starting resources**: 2w, 15c
- **Starting ability**: Must use **Golden Spades** to transform terrain. Cannot use regular Spades — instead gain 1 Priest per regular Spade received. When taking Transform and Build, buy Golden Spades at 4 Coins each. Each Golden Spade also grants +1 VP and +1 Power.
- **Stronghold**:
  - Ability: Golden Spades now cost **3 Coins** (instead of 4). Special action: take 1 Coin per opponent Trading Post on the board.

### Time Travelers (Plains) — BGA: "timetravellers"
- **Starting ability**: Do **not** score VP from the current round's Scoring tile. Instead score VP from **both** the previous round's and next round's Scoring tiles. Round 1 uses round 6 as "previous"; round 6 uses round 1 as "next". If both tiles reward the same action, gain both. Cult bonuses still come from the current round's tile. Scoring tiles are not flipped face-down.
- **Temple cost**: 4w+8c (non-standard)
- **Sanctuary cost**: 4w+8c

### Treasurers (Wasteland) — BGA: "treasurers"
- **Starting power**: 4/8
- **Starting resources**: 4w, 15c
- **Starting ability**: In Phase I Income, may choose to put any resources (Coins, Workers, Priests) into **Treasury** (separate storage, cannot be used). At the start of each round, before Income, take all Treasury resources and **double** them onto your faction board.
- **Stronghold**:
  - Ability: May put resources into Treasury whenever received (not just Phase I). Must decide at the moment of receiving. Doubling from Treasury does not count as "receiving resources" for this ability.

### Wisps (Lakes) — BGA: "wisps"
- **Starting power**: 7/5
- **Starting ability**: Whenever you build a Trading Post, may immediately transform 1 directly adjacent terrain with exactly 1 free Spade (no buying additional Spades, no building on that space).
- **Stronghold**:
  - On build: gain 7 VP and place 1 free Dwelling on any unoccupied Lakes space (no cost, ignore adjacency, must have been Lakes at start of this action)
