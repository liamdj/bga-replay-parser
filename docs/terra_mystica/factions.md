# Terra Mystica Base Game Factions

All differences from standard building costs and income. Factions not listed under a section have no differences for that aspect (other than stronghold ability).

Starting power is 12 tokens across Bowl I/II (Bowl III starts empty). Standard: 5/7. Exceptions noted per faction.

## Halflings (Plains)
- **Starting power**: 3/9
- **Starting ability**: +1 VP per Spade
- **Exchange Track advance cost**: 2w+1c+1p (instead of 2w+5c+1p)
- **Stronghold**:
  - Cost: 4w+8c
  - Income: 4pw
  - Ability: 3 Spades to apply immediately (once)

## Cultists (Plains)
- **Starting ability**: Advance 1 cult when opponent takes Power from your building
- **Stronghold**:
  - Income: 4pw
  - Ability: +7 VP (once)

## Alchemists (Swamp)
- **Starting ability**: Trade 1 VP → 1c or 2c → 1 VP
- **Trading House income**: 2c+1pw, 2c+1pw, 3c+1pw, 4c+1pw
- **Stronghold**:
  - Income: 6c
  - On build: charge 12pw
  - Ability: +2 Power per Spade used

## Darklings (Swamp)
- **Starting ability**: Pay Priests instead of Workers for terraforming, +2 VP per step
- **Sanctuary**:
  - Cost: 4w+10c
  - Income: 2p (instead of 1p)
- **Stronghold**:
  - Income: 4pw
  - Ability: Convert up to 3 Workers to Priests (once)

## Witches (Forest)
- **Starting ability**: +5 VP per Town
- **Stronghold**:
  - Income: 2pw
  - Ability: Build 1 free Dwelling ignoring adjacency on any free Forest (special action)

## Auren (Forest)
- **Sanctuary cost**: 4w+8c
- **Stronghold**:
  - Cost: 4w+8c
  - Income: 2pw
  - On build: immediately gain 1 Favor tile (one-time; advance its cult track as normal)
  - Ability: advance 2 on a cult track (special action)

## Mermaids (Lakes)
- **Starting power**: 3/9
- **Starting ability**: Skip 1 River when founding a Town
- **Shipping track**: starts at 1 shipping. Can upgrade to max 5 shipping granting 2/3/4/5 VPs (instead of max 3)
- **Sanctuary cost**: 4w+8c
- **Stronghold**:
  - Income: 4pw
  - Ability: Advance 1 Shipping for free (once)

## Swarmlings (Lakes)
- **Starting power**: 3/9
- **Starting ability**: Expensive buildings. +3w when founding a Town
- **Dwelling**:
  - Cost: 2w+3c
  - Income: standard, but starts with 2w basic income
- **Trading House**: 
  - Cost: 3w+4c (8c if no neighbors)
  - Income: first 3 produce 2c+2pw; 4th produces 3c+2pw
- **Temple**:
  - Cost: 3w+6c
- **Sanctuary**:
  - Cost: 5w+8c
  - Income: 2p
- **Stronghold**:
  - Cost: 5w+8c
  - Income: 4pw
  - Ability: Upgrade Dwelling to Trading House free (special action)

## Dwarves (Mountains)
- **Starting ability**: Tunneling — skip terrain/river for 2w, +4 VP each time, no Shipping
- **Trading House income**: 3c+1pw, 2c+1pw, 2c+2pw, 3c+2pw
- **Stronghold**:
  - Income: 2pw
  - Ability: Tunnel for 1 Worker (instead of 2)

## Engineers (Mountains)
- **Starting power**: 3/9
- **Starting ability**: Cheap buildings. Can build Bridges for 2w.
- **Dwelling**:
  - Cost: 1w+1c
  - Income: w, w, 0, w, w, 0, w, w (no basic worker income)
- **Trading House cost**: 1w+2c (4c if not directly adjacent)
- **Temple**: 
  - Cost: 1w+4c
  - Income: 1st: p 2nd: 5pw 3rd: p
- **Stronghold**:
  - Cost: 3w+6c
  - Income: 2pw
  - Ability: +3 VP per Bridge between 2 of your structures when passing
- **Sanctuary cost**: 3w+6c

## Nomads (Desert)
- **Starting ability**: Start with 3 Dwellings
- **Trading House income**: 2c+1pw, 2c+1pw, 3c+1pw, 4c+1pw
- **Stronghold**:
  - Cost: 4w+8c
  - Income: 4pw
  - Ability: Sandstorm — transform adjacent terrain to Home terrain (not across bridge) (special action)

## Giants (Wasteland)
- **Starting ability**: Always pay exactly 2 Spades to terraform
- **Stronghold**:
  - Income: 4pw
  - Ability: 2 free Spades (special action)

## Fakirs (Desert)
- **Starting power**: 7/5
- **Starting ability**: Carpet Flight — skip terrain/river for 1 Priest, +4 VP each time, no Shipping
- **Stronghold**:
  - Cost: 4w+10c
  - Income: 1p
  - Ability: +1 Carpet Flight range

## Chaos Magicians (Wasteland)
- **Starting ability**: Start with 1 Dwelling (placed last). Get 2 Favor tiles per Temple/Sanctuary
- **Sanctuary cost**: 4w+8c
- **Stronghold**:
  - Cost: 4w+4c
  - Income: 2w
  - Ability: Double-turn — take two consecutive actions (special action)
